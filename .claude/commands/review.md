Weekly review with Pouls Coach, following the "Weekly review" section of `get_method`:

1. The plan (`get_journal`) against what happened (`list_workouts` since the plan's Monday, my
   session notes and check-ins). Mention how fresh the export is (`data_through`).
2. Habits: `habit_progress`; add one new habit only if the current ones hold.
3. Weight: `get_weight` (7-day average and rate).
4. Recovery: `get_recovery`.
5. What went well first, then next week's plan: `check_plan`, then `save_plan` with a short
   rationale, and `add_note` for anything you learned about me.
