Daily check-in with Pouls Coach. Follow `get_method` (section "Daily check-in") and `get_journal`.

In one short message, ask which habits I kept, my weight if I weighed, energy and soreness (1-5) and
how I slept. Save my answers with `log_checkin` (and `log_session` if I trained). Then call
`check_readiness` and tell me today's session from the current plan, or an easier alternative, with
the reason in one line.
